Slateport: market stalls stand up, harbour sailboats stop being cards

What you saw: the market stalls were knee-high blue slabs hovering over the paving, and the moored
sailboats in the harbour were a flat card of sail standing on a slab of hull.

Why - stalls: the awning pin gave the cloth a height of 13 px (a bench) and its hip roof rose at half a
pixel per pixel of run, so a 32 px stall became a steep 8 px tent. Nothing carried the cloth: the
drawing's posts were painted on the roof instead of standing under it.

Why - boats: Gen3Boat built the sails as one 1x1x1 box per art pixel at a single z - a card - so from any
angle but the front the sail vanished edge-on, and the hull's side band was 7 px, mostly below the quay.

Now - stalls: the cloth stands at 18 px with its rise capped at 2.5 px (a shallow canopy, not a tent), and
four posts at the stall's corners carry it.

Now - boats: the mast is a pole, and the cloth is one continuous surface per sail: the silhouette is still
the drawing's pixel outline, but the sheet bellies 4 px out to leeward, so a turned camera sees a sail with
wind in it. The hull's side is 8 px, so the boat shows above the waterline.

Checked in QA at the market and the harbour (level 4, 0 fallbacks). Still to do in Slateport: the market
wares are painted into the ground tiles (they need cutting out and building as props), and the shipyard and
fanclub models are only about half as deep as their footprints with open backs (shipyard 71 of 112 px,
fanclub 39 of 80 px) - both are separate jobs.
